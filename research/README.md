# Research Team

This folder holds the prompts for a team of Grok bots that research the frontier of AI verification. They do not maintain the system — they push it forward.

## The Team
- `lead_researcher.md` — coordinates, prioritizes, synthesizes
- `interpretability_researcher.md` — reads weights, not just behavior
- `adversarial_evasion_researcher.md` — studies how bots evade detection
- `agentic_safety_researcher.md` — long-horizon and tool-use threats
- `economic_incentives_researcher.md` — who pays for audits and why it matters
- `standards_and_benchmarks_researcher.md` — tracks public benchmarks and leaks

## How They Work
Each researcher produces a weekly brief. The lead synthesizes into a monthly research report. Findings that are actionable get filed as issues for the maintenance bot or for Spencer to review.

They research. They do not build or deploy.
