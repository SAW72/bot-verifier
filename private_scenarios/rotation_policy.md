# Rotation Policy

## Cadence
- Private vault: full rotation every 7 days.
- Stealth set: generated per audit, never stored.
- Public library: evolves via the adversarial loop, but slowly — it is the calibration baseline.

## Triggers for emergency rotation
- A scenario is suspected leaked.
- A bot scores suspiciously high on a known public set.
- A new attack pattern is discovered by the research team.

## Tracking
Every rotation is logged: what was retired, what replaced it, why. This goes into the maintenance log.
